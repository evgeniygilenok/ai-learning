What you will demonstrate

  • Deny arguments that attempt to widen authority

Invalid and unauthorized proposals produce visible denials, and model arguments cannot grant access.

Understand the mechanism

A tool contract needs more than a JSON schema. The server must validate arguments, bind a trusted caller and tenant, check scope, and define failure behavior. The model may supply a tenant argument for validation. It cannot replace the tenant stored in trusted session context.

lookupTransfer recognizes tr_001 and returns not-found for other synthetic IDs. That distinction prevents a missing record from turning into a fabricated pending transfer. draftEscalation requires the narrow write scope, human approval, and an idempotency key for execution.

Worked example

A valid lookup asks for tr_001 in demo-bank and receives pending with three business days elapsed. Changing tenantId to another-bank produces unauthorized. Adding approved:true to the arguments produces invalid_arguments. Neither proposal changes syntheticToolContext or a saved job's scopes.

Guided build · 30 minutes

  1. Write the input, success output, and possible errors for both tools. Locate each enforcement check in tools.ts.
  2. Exercise missing scope, wrong tenant, unknown property, and missing transfer with deterministic proposals.
  3. Improve one confusing error or result contract and compare the agent's behavior on the same case.

Your task · 15 minutes

Add a learner test where a model puts a requested scope inside its arguments. Show an explicit denial and unchanged trusted context.

Failure investigation · 10 minutes

A proposal uses a transfer ID that does not exist. Inspect not-found and prove no draft effect follows. Do not repair the test by making every lookup return pending.

Before running, predict the state, evidence, and effect count. Then compare your prediction with the record and name the responsible layer.

Exit explanation · 20 minutes

Who supplies actor and scope? Why is passing schema validation insufficient to authorize a tool?
