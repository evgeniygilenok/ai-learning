# Product Brief

## User and job

Synthetic support agents need a fast, cited explanation of policy and a controlled path to draft escalations without exposing another tenant's data or executing an action implicitly.

## Success and failure

- Success: correct triage contract, authorized evidence, useful citation, deliberate escalation.
- Must refuse/escalate: missing evidence, identity ambiguity, authorization failure, policy conflict, or risky action without approval.
- Measures: contract pass rate, retrieval recall/precision by slice, citation validity, approval integrity, latency, cost, and user task success.

## Non-goals

No real financial advice, real customer data, autonomous transactions, or legal/compliance determination.
