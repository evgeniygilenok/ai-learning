19. Start work from events

15 minutes explanation · 55 minutes building · 20 minutes review

Prerequisites: practice-18.

What you will demonstrate

  • Use stable event IDs, bounded retries, cancellation, and approval pauses
  • Recover queued work without widening authority

Duplicate intake creates no additional logical escalation, the worker stops cleanly, and paused work resumes with its original authority.

Understand the mechanism

The add command is a local event source. Event IDs are stable, and repeating an ID with the same case returns the existing job. Reusing an ID for different content is an explicit conflict. work processes a finite batch, skips undecided approval pauses, and exits. A later invocation retries eligible transient failures.

Workers claim a five-minute lease and check ownership before saving state or effects. SIGINT and SIGTERM stop intake after the current bounded call finishes. Cancellation applies to idle work, preserves existing effects, and never pretends to undo an action. A crashed worker's lease expires before another worker resumes it.

Worked example

Add ordinary and missing-evidence cases, then repeat ordinary's exact intake. work 5 processes only two logical jobs. The ordinary case pauses for approval; the missing-evidence case fails with no effect. Approve the ordinary case, then work again. Its stored scopes remain the ones saved at intake.

Guided build · 30 minutes

  1. Inspect intake deduplication, retry scheduling, cancellation, and worker claims in the implementation.
  2. Run a short batch including a duplicate and an approval pause. Stop the worker cleanly and inspect every saved state.
  3. Inject a transient provider error and a process interruption. Resume after the retry time or lease expiry and compare effect counts.

Your task · 15 minutes

Improve one worker operating behavior in your learner checkout, such as a concise retry summary. Keep stable IDs, saved authority, and the batch bound intact.

Failure investigation · 10 minutes

Two processes try to claim the same case. One receives busy or skips it. After lease takeover, the former owner must fail its write check rather than overwrite the new owner's state.

Before running, predict the state, evidence, and effect count. Then compare your prediction with the record and name the responsible layer.

Exit explanation · 20 minutes

What does duplicate intake mean, when is retry allowed, and what happens when shutdown arrives during an approved case?

Explain the result in your own words and link the supporting command, code change, or redacted record. A missing prerequisite uses the next session's time.
