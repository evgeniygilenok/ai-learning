20. Improve behavior without silent promotion

15 minutes explanation · 55 minutes building · 20 minutes review

Prerequisites: practice-19.

Record the result in module-4.md#improvement-decision.

What you will demonstrate

  • Compare a proposed change on a preserved evaluation pack
  • Keep human promotion and rollback explicit

A human can approve or reject the comparison, restore the prior behavior, and verify that permissions did not expand.

Understand the mechanism

Choose one recorded failure and propose a change to context, prompt, or workflow. Save the previous version before editing. Run both versions on the same case pack, including boundary cases. A quality improvement that breaks rejected-approval behavior is not an acceptable trade here.

The system can prepare a candidate and its comparison. The learner decides whether to promote it. Record the tradeoff and the command or commit needed to restore the previous version. A model's recommendation cannot grant new scopes or modify the saved approval policy.

Worked example

The baseline explanation omits the policy source ID. The candidate requires a cited uncertainty statement and rejects an absent citation in the final projection. Compare both on answerable, missing-policy, and denied-access cases. Accept only if the named grounding problem improves and the authorization outcomes remain correct. If extra refusals make the product less useful, reject the candidate and preserve the finding.

Guided build · 30 minutes

  1. Select a genuine failure from the module evidence and save the prior behavior version.
  2. Implement one candidate change in your learner checkout. Compare it with the baseline using the same deterministic cases and bounded real runs.
  3. Review the tradeoff, decide on promotion, and demonstrate rollback. Update the runbook and architecture notes if behavior changed.

Your task · 15 minutes

Prepare a candidate improvement that the learner rejects. Explain why preserving that decision is useful evidence rather than a failed course outcome.

Failure investigation · 10 minutes

A candidate produces better explanations but changes default scopes or approves its own draft. Reject it regardless of the quality score and show the unchanged authority contract.

Before running, predict the state, evidence, and effect count. Then compare your prediction with the record and name the responsible layer.

Exit explanation · 20 minutes

What improved, what regressed, who promoted the change, and how do you restore the prior behavior?

Explain the result in your own words and link the supporting command, code change, or redacted record. A missing prerequisite uses the next session's time.
